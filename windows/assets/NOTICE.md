# Third-party assets

- `fonts/UbuntuSans-Regular.ttf`, `fonts/UbuntuSans-Bold.ttf`: static instances (Latin subset) of
  **Ubuntu Sans** by Canonical/Dalton Maag, under the Ubuntu Font Licence 1.0 —
  see `fonts/LICENCE-fonts-ubuntu.txt`.
- `icons/*.svg`: symbolic icons from the **Adwaita icon theme** (GNOME Project), recolored white
  for tinting, under CC-BY-SA 3.0/4.0 or LGPL-3 — see `icons/COPYING-adwaita-icon-theme.txt`.
